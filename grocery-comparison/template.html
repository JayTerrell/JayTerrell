How these prices were built

  • This environment can't reach the store websites directly, so no live prices were scraped.
  • Walmart store-brand prices are the anchor, set to Aug 2026 national averages (BLS: eggs $2.14/dozen, ground beef $6.83/lb, chicken breast $4.18/lb).
  • Other stores are modeled from each chain's typical price level by aisle, with per-item variation. Expect any single item to be off by 10–20%. The store ranking is more reliable than any single price.
