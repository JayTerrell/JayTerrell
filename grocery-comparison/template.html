How to read the totals

  • "Same items everywhere" totals only the items that every fully priced store carries, so the stores are compared on an identical list.
  • Meat is priced per pound for your quantity. Stores sell fixed packs (often 4–5 lb), so the checkout amount can be higher.
  • Prices are regular prices. Sales, digital coupons and loyalty prices are not included.

Not included

  • Pickup fees. Aldi and Sprouts pickup runs through Instacart, which may add a small fee.
  • Sales tax. Most groceries are tax-exempt in Texas; household items are taxed at 8.25%.

Before you order

  • Prices change weekly. Build the cart in your top store's app to confirm the total.
  • If Aldi is your store, plan a quick second stop for the few items it doesn't carry.
