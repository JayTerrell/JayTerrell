    About the win rates

    Results are approximate master-level figures (players rated 2200+, over-the-board), rounded to whole percent, as reported by databases such as the Lichess Masters explorer. They were not pulled live. Use the Lichess link on each position for current numbers.

    Why a child can outnumber its parent

    Game counts belong to the position, not the move order. 1.c4 e6 2.Nc3 d5 3.d4 is the Queen's Gambit Declined, so it carries the QGD's game count even though few games start with 1.c4 e6.

    Reading the bars

    Light is a White win, grey a draw, dark a Black win. "Your score" counts a win as 1 and a draw as ½ for the colour you picked.
