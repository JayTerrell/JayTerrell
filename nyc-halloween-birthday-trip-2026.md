# NYC Halloween Birthday Trip: Robert Glasper at the Blue Note

**Dates:** Thu Oct 29 to Sun Nov 1, 2026 (3 nights)
**From:** Frisco, TX
**Goal:** Robert Glasper X Halloween with Derrick Hodge & Chris Dave, Blue Note Jazz Club (131 W 3rd St), Sat Oct 31, 10:30 PM show
**Rules:** Cheapest options, stay at a Hilton for points, no rental car, no Spirit

> Prices below are estimates pulled on Sep 26, 2026. They change daily, so check them live before booking.

---

## 1. Concert tickets (buy first, no refunds)

- **Show:** 10:30 PM (doors 10:00 PM). There's also an 8:00 PM show, but the Village Halloween Parade runs on 6th Ave from 7 to 11 PM, a block from the club. **Get the 10:30 show**: watch the parade first, then walk over.
- **Price:** from about $89 plus fees, and a **$20 food/drink minimum** per person.
- **Seating:** first come, first served. The **bar area** is usually the cheapest ticket but is 21+ and turns into standing room once full. Get there at 10:00 when doors open.
- **Buy:** [TicketWeb](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/robert-glasper-x-halloween-with-blue-note-jazz-club-tickets/15019023) / [Blue Note event page](https://www.bluenotejazz.com/nyc/tm-event/robert-glasper-x-halloween-with-derrick-hodge-chris-dave/)
- This is the last weekend of "Robtober" (Oct 1 to Nov 1) and Halloween sells out fast. **Book this today.**

## 2. Flights: Southwest, Dallas Love Field (DAL) to LaGuardia (LGA)

| | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Airline | **Southwest nonstop DAL to LGA** (about 3h15m, many daily flights) |
| Out | Thu Oct 29, a morning/midday flight (usually cheaper than evening) |
| Back | Sun Nov 1, around 5 to 7 PM from LGA, landing DAL around 8 to 10 PM |
| Est. fare | **~$200 to $300 round trip** (Basic/Wanna Get Away) |
| Why | Love Field is closer to Frisco than DFW, it's nonstop, and LGA is the easiest NYC airport without a car |

**Backups to compare (no Spirit):** American DFW to LGA (Basic Economy), Delta DFW to LGA/JFK. Search both on [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-dallas-to-new-york.html) with *Spirit unchecked* under Airlines.

**Money savers**
- Pack **carry-on only**. Southwest now charges for checked bags, and a carry-on plus personal item is free.
- **Clocks fall back 1 hour at 2 AM Sun Nov 1** (end of daylight saving time). Double-check your return flight time on your phone.

## 3. Hotel: Hilton, booked direct for points

**Pick A: Hilton Garden Inn New York/Tribeca** (39 Avenue of the Americas)
- About 15 to 20 minutes' walk, or a short subway ride, to the Blue Note, and right at the parade's starting point (Canal St and 6th Ave)
- Walk back after the show, no late-night train or Uber needed
- Estimated **$230 to $330 per night** with tax on Halloween weekend, so **~$700 to $1,000 for 3 nights**

**Pick B (cheapest): Hilton Garden Inn or Hampton Inn, Long Island City, Queens**
- Usually **$60 to $120 per night cheaper**, so **~$480 to $700 for 3 nights**
- One subway stop from Manhattan, and closer to LGA
- Downside: about 25 to 30 minutes on the subway home after a show that ends around midnight

**My pick:** if Pick B saves you $200 or more for the stay, take it. Otherwise pay a little extra for Tribeca so you can walk home on your birthday night.

**Hilton points tips**
- Book on **hilton.com or the Hilton Honors app**. Expedia and Hotels.com bookings usually **don't earn points** or count toward status.
- Sign in first so you get the **Honors member discount** (often the lowest rate).
- Pick a cancellable rate if it's only a few dollars more, so you can rebook if the price drops.
- Use the app for digital check-in and key. Ask about late checkout on Sunday or leave bags at the front desk.

## 4. Getting around without a car

| Leg | How | Est. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Frisco to Love Field and back | Uber/Lyft (~30 to 40 min), or a friend drop-off | ~$35 to $55 each way |
| LGA to hotel | **Free Q70 "LaGuardia Link" bus** to Jackson Hts/Woodside, then subway (E/F/M/7). Tribeca: ~50 to 60 min. LIC: ~35 to 45 min | ~$3 |
| Around NYC | Subway/bus, tap your phone or card with **OMNY**. Fares cap after 12 rides in 7 days, so the rest of the week rides free | ~$25 to $35 total |
| Blue Note | Subway to **W 4th St-Washington Sq** (A/C/E/B/D/F/M), 2-minute walk | included |

Halloween night tip: 6th Ave is closed and packed from 7 to 11 PM, and the club is on the east side of 6th Ave. Cross early or go around the parade route. Leave extra time.

## 5. Day-by-day plan

**Thu Oct 29: Travel day**
- Uber to Love Field, then fly to LGA
- Q70 bus plus subway to the hotel and check in
- Cheap dinner: Joe's Pizza (Carmine St), Mamoun's Falafel, or the food stalls in Chinatown

**Fri Oct 30: Explore (mostly free)**
- Walk the **High Line**, then **Chelsea Market** (lunch)
- **Little Island** and the Hudson River waterfront at sunset
- Or: the Staten Island Ferry (free), with views of the Statue of Liberty
- Evening: Washington Square Park, then drinks in the West Village

**Sat Oct 31: Birthday + Halloween 🎃🎂**
- Sleep in, then birthday brunch
- Afternoon: SoHo, Washington Square Park, Greenwich Village
- 7 to 9 PM: watch (or march in, if you're in costume) the **Village Halloween Parade** on 6th Ave. Anyone in costume can join at Canal St and 6th Ave between 6:30 and 9 PM
- 10:00 PM: arrive at the Blue Note for doors
- **10:30 PM: Robert Glasper X Halloween** 🎹
- Walk or ride back to the hotel

**Sun Nov 1: Fly home**
- Check out (store bags at the front desk), then a last brunch
- Leave the hotel **about 3 hours before your flight** (transit to LGA takes about an hour)
- Fly LGA to DAL, then Uber home
